### Zero-Knowledge Architecture

Your security is our top priority. That's why we built our Password Manager with **zero-knowledge architecture**:

- **Client-Side Encryption:** Your data is encrypted in your browser before it ever reaches our servers
- **Master Password Never Transmitted:** We never see or store your master password
- **AES-256-GCM Encryption:** Military-grade encryption protects your data
- **PBKDF2 Key Derivation:** 100,000+ iterations make brute-force attacks impractical

**What this means:** Even if our servers were compromised, your passwords would remain secure. We literally cannot access your data.

2. Click "Create Account"

4. Create a strong master password (12+ characters)

- This is the ONLY way to recover your account
- We cannot reset your password without it
- Store it offline (printed or written down)

**Step 3: Add Your First Password**
1. Click "Add Credential"
2. Enter the website name and URL
3. Enter your username
4. Enter or generate a password
5. Click "Save"

**Step 4: Install on Other Devices**
1. Visit the website on your other devices
2. Log in with your master password
3. Your vault will sync automatically

- Enable two-factor authentication
