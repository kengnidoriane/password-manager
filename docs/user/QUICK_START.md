# Quick Start Guide

Get up and running with Password Manager in 5 minutes!

## Step 1: Create Your Account (2 minutes)

1. **Open Password Manager** in your web browser
2. **Click "Create Account"**
3. **Enter your email address**
4. **Create a strong master password**
   - Minimum 12 characters
   - Include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols
   - Example: `MySecure#Pass2026!`
   - ⚠️ **Remember this password** - you'll need it every time you login
5. **Save your recovery key**
   - A unique key appears after registration
   - **Copy it and store it safely** (not in the password manager!)
   - You'll need this if you forget your master password
6. **Click "Complete Registration"**

✅ Your account is ready!

---

## Step 2: Add Your First Password (1 minute)

1. **Click the "+ Add Credential" button**
2. **Fill in the details:**
   - **Title**: "Gmail" (or any name you'll recognize)
   - **Website**: https://gmail.com
   - **Username**: your.email@gmail.com
   - **Password**: Your Gmail password (or click "Generate" for a new one)
3. **Click "Save"**

✅ Your first password is securely stored and encrypted!

---

## Step 3: Install on Your Device (1 minute)

### On Desktop (Chrome, Edge, Brave)
1. Look for the **install icon** (⊕) in the address bar
2. Click it and select **"Install"**
3. The app opens in its own window

### On iPhone/iPad
1. Tap the **Share button** (square with arrow)
2. Scroll and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
3. Tap **"Add"**

### On Android
1. Tap the **three-dot menu**
2. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install App"**
3. Tap **"Add"**

✅ Now you can access Password Manager like a native app!

---

## Step 4: Access on Another Device (1 minute)

1. **Open Password Manager** on your other device (phone, tablet, another computer)
2. **Click "Login"**
3. **Enter your email and master password**
4. **Wait for sync** (usually takes a few seconds)

✅ All your passwords are now available on this device too!

## Quick Tips

### 🔐 Using Your Passwords

**To copy a password:**
1. Find the credential in your vault
2. Click the **copy icon** (📋) next to the password
3. Paste it where you need it
4. The clipboard auto-clears after 60 seconds for security

**To view a password:**
- Click the **eye icon** (👁️) to reveal/hide the password

### 🎲 Generate Strong Passwords

1. Click **"Password Generator"** in the menu
2. Adjust the length (we recommend 16+ characters)
3. Select character types (uppercase, lowercase, numbers, symbols)
4. Click **"Generate"**
5. Click **"Copy"** or **"Save to Vault"**

### 🔍 Find Passwords Quickly

- Use the **search bar** at the top
- Type any part of the website name, URL, or username
- Results appear instantly

### 📁 Organize Your Passwords

**Create folders:**
1. Click **"New Folder"** in the sidebar
2. Name it (e.g., "Work", "Personal", "Banking")
3. Drag credentials into folders (or select folder when editing)

**Use tags:**
- Add tags when creating/editing credentials
- Click tags to filter your vault

### 🔒 Lock Your Vault

- Click the **lock icon** in the toolbar
- Or wait 15 minutes of inactivity (auto-lock)
- Enter your master password to unlock

## Next Steps

### Enhance Your Security

1. **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**
   - Go to Settings → Security
   - Click "Enable 2FA"
   - Scan QR code with Google Authenticator or Authy
   - Save your backup codes

2. **Check Your Security Dashboard**
   - Click "Security" in the menu
   - See weak, reused, or breached passwords
   - Update any flagged passwords

3. **Enable Biometric Login** (if available)
   - Go to Settings → Security
   - Click "Enable Biometric Authentication"
   - Use fingerprint or face recognition for quick access

### Import Existing Passwords

1. **Export from your current password manager**
   - Chrome: chrome://settings/passwords → Export
   - Firefox: about:logins → Export
   - LastPass, 1Password, etc.: Check their export options

2. **Import to Password Manager**
   - Go to Settings → Data Management
   - Click "Import Credentials"
   - Select your CSV file
   - Review and confirm import

### Customize Your Experience

- **Change theme**: Settings → Appearance → Theme (Light/Dark/Auto)
- **Adjust timeouts**: Settings → Security → Session Timeout
- **Set clipboard timeout**: Settings → Security → Clipboard Auto-Clear

## Common Questions

**Q: What if I forget my master password?**
A: Use your recovery key to reset it. Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password", then enter your recovery key.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes! All data is encrypted with AES-256 before leaving your device. The server only stores encrypted data and cannot access your passwords.

**Q: Can I use this offline?**
A: Yes! Once you've logged in, you can access all your passwords offline. Changes sync automatically when you're back online.

**Q: How do I share a password with someone?**
A: Open the credential, click "Share", enter their email (they need a Password Manager account), and set permissions.

**Q: What happens if I delete a password?**
A: It moves to the trash folder for 30 days. You can restore it anytime during this period. After 30 days, it's permanently deleted.
